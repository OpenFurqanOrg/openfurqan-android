# What's new in OpenFurqan for Android

## 1.0.7 — 13 November 2026
### New
- **Tajweed, taught as lessons:** hear each rule in three ayahs with only that rule coloured, then
  find it yourself in a new ayah. No score and no "wrong".
- **Daily Sunnah follows the day:** the reading for now comes first, with why it is read and the
  hadith that tells of it, and a quiet "Read tonight" once you have read it.
- **Translations in 17 more languages:** Afar, Asante Twi, Belarusian, Catalan, Cebuano, Chewa,
  Dagbani, Fulah, Ganda, Georgian, Iranun, Kannada, Lingala, Luhya, Malagasy, Shona and Zulu, for
  92 languages in all.
- **Al-Mukhtasar in Arabic, Persian, Indonesian and Russian,** for 48 tafsirs in all.

### Changed
- **A plainer reader header** that steps aside while you read, and becomes the player while a
  recitation plays.
- **Practice is calmer:** one screen with Start, clearer buttons, each word said aloud, and a kind
  ending to every session. Activity shows your week and what you know by heart, without pressure.
- Learn leads with Duas, and the note stays on the ayah's page.

## 1.0.6 — 7 November 2026
### New
- **Study, rebuilt:** it opens on Continue, with today's Sunnah reading and a word of the day. Each
  ayah is one page with its Words, Tafsir, Tajweed and Hadith, where you can compare translations
  and tafsirs, repeat the recitation and keep a note.
- **The Quran's duas as a book:** 88 duas, laid out like a printed page, by surah or by need.

### Fixed
- In Practice, studying a word's root brings you back to your card.

## 1.0.5 — 3 November 2026
### Changed
- **New tab bar icons:** solid symbols for Read, Study and Practice, with the selected tab shown in colour.
- The reader's long-press menu icons are refined to match.

## 1.0.4 — 1 November 2026
### New
- **Go straight to any ayah:** tap the reader's header to open the Ayah picker at the verse you're on.
  Choose an ayah with the same number and slider controls as Page, and it opens below the header.

## 1.0.3 — 30 October 2026
### Changed
- **The tab bar shows where you are:** the selected tab's icon fills, the others stay outlined.
- **A deeper teal app icon,** matching the store artwork.
- Contact moves to contact@openfurqan.com.

## 1.0.2 — 20 October 2026
### New
- **Translations in 66 more languages:** 91 new translations, from Indonesian, Persian and Russian to
  Swahili, Tamil and Chinese, for 75 languages in all.
- **21 new tafsirs:** Ibn Kathir and Tazkirul Quran in English; al-Muyassar, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and
  al-Wasit in Arabic; as-Sa'di in Russian; and Al-Mukhtasar in 13 more languages.
- **Search in the translation and tafsir lists:** find a language by its English name, by its name in the
  app's language or by its own name, or find an edition by its translator.
- **Choose your app icon:** Teal, Black, Deep Purple, Deep Blue or Deep Green, in Settings → App icon.

### Changed
- The language lists start with English, Urdu, Bangla, Hindi and Indonesian, then go A to Z.

## 1.0.1 — 16 October 2026
### New
- **Save image:** save an ayah's image card to your photos.
- **Saved ayahs** has its own page in Study. Swipe to remove one, with Undo.
- **Every Quranic root has a page:** all 1,651 of them, 526 new, each led by its meaning.

### Changed
- **A new share card:** the ayah large and centred, in its own script, with the OpenFurqan mark.
- **Corrections** to the built-in Bangla, Hindi and English translations.
- Study's old Library gave way to Saved ayahs.

### Fixed
- Vocabulary: words spelled alike but from different roots are no longer mixed together.

## 1.0.0 — 13 October 2026
The first release, to testers on Google Play.
- Read by Surah, Juz or the 604-page Mushaf, with translations, tafsir and word-by-word meanings.
- Recitation that follows along, with repeat and background play.
- Study any word's grammar and root, hadith beside the ayahs, and a notebook.
- Memorize ayahs and vocabulary with daily review.
- In English, Bangla, Hindi and Urdu. No account, no ads, no tracking.

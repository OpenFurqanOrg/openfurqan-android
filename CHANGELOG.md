# What's new in OpenFurqan for Android

## Coming next
- A deeper teal app icon, matching the store artwork
- Contact moves to contact@openfurqan.com

## 1.0.2 — 20 October 2026
### New
- **Translations in 66 more languages:** 91 new translations, from Indonesian, Persian and Russian to
  Swahili, Tamil and Chinese, for 75 languages in all.
- **21 new tafsirs:** Ibn Kathir and Tazkirul Quran in English; al-Muyassar, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and
  al-Wasit in Arabic; as-Sa'di in Russian; and Al-Mukhtasar in 13 more languages.
- **Search in the translation and tafsir lists:** find a language by its English name, by its name in the
  app's language or by its own name, or find an edition by its translator.
- **Choose your app icon:** Teal, Black, Deep Purple, Deep Blue or Deep Green, in Settings → App icon.

### Changed
- The language lists start with English, Urdu, Bangla, Hindi and Indonesian, then go A to Z.

## 1.0.1 — 16 October 2026
### New
- **Save image:** save an ayah's image card to your photos.
- **Saved ayahs** has its own page in Study. Swipe to remove one, with Undo.
- **Every Quranic root has a page:** all 1,651 of them, 526 new, each led by its meaning.

### Changed
- **A new share card:** the ayah large and centred, in its own script, with the OpenFurqan mark.
- **Corrections** to the built-in Bangla, Hindi and English translations.
- Study's old Library gave way to Saved ayahs.

### Fixed
- Vocabulary: words spelled alike but from different roots are no longer mixed together.

## 1.0.0 — 13 October 2026
The first release, to testers on Google Play.
- Read by Surah, Juz or the 604-page Mushaf, with translations, tafsir and word-by-word meanings.
- Recitation that follows along, with repeat and background play.
- Study any word's grammar and root, hadith beside the ayahs, and a notebook.
- Memorize ayahs and vocabulary with daily review.
- In English, Bangla, Hindi and Urdu. No account, no ads, no tracking.
